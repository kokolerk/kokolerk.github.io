# About Me

Hello! 😊 I'm a third-year PhD candidate at [The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK)](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/) in the [Department of Computer Science and Engineering](https://www.cse.cuhk.edu.hk/), supervised by [Prof. James Cheng](https://www.cse.cuhk.edu.hk/~jcheng/). My research interests include Agentic Reinforcement Learning, Visual Language Models, and Machine Learning Theory. 

On the application side, I am interested in building the efficient and effective agent systems, especially in real-world, multi-turn settings using a reinforcement learning approach. On the theory side, I have done research on graph neural networks and attention mechanisms supervised by [Dr. Yifei Wang](https://yifeiwang77.com/) at [MIT CSAIL](https://www.csail.mit.edu/). I have also collaborated closely with [Dr. Xinyi Wu](https://xinyiwu98.github.io/) at [MIT IDSS](https://idss.mit.edu/) and [Dr. Kevin Qinghong Lin](https://qhlin.me/) at [Oxford Torr Visual Group](https://torrvision.com/index.html).


Prior to coming to CUHK, I was an undergraduate student at Harbin Institute of Technology, where I worked as a research intern at [SCIR](https://ir.hit.edu.cn/), supervised by [Prof. Libo Qin](https://faculty.csu.edu.cn/qinlibo/zh_CN/index/204695/list/index.htm).
